# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** April 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how "TicTacHelmets" ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Player nickname:** When you play online or Bluetooth-nearby matches, you may enter a display name. This name is transmitted to the other player only for the duration of the match and is not tied to any real-world identity.
- **Room code:** Online matches use a short randomly-generated room code. The code exists on our servers only while the match is active.
- **Move data:** During an online or Bluetooth match, the positions of your moves are exchanged with your opponent so both players see the same board.

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Bluetooth scanning:** When you choose the "Nearby" game mode, the App scans for nearby Bluetooth Low Energy devices running TicTacHelmets. No persistent device identifiers are stored; scan results are used only to show the list of discoverable opponents and are discarded when you leave the mode.
- **Approximate location (Android only, Bluetooth mode only):** Android requires location permission to perform Bluetooth LE scans. The App does not read, store, or transmit your location — the permission is requested solely to unblock Bluetooth discovery on older Android versions.

### Third-Party Services

#### Google AdMob
The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process certain information including:
- Device identifiers (advertising ID)
- IP address
- General location (country/region level)
- App usage data

Users who purchase the "Remove Ads" in-app product no longer see ads after purchase.

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

#### Firebase Realtime Database
Online (remote) multiplayer match data — room code, player nicknames, and move history — is stored on Firebase Realtime Database so both opponents share a consistent view of the board. This data is:
- Stored only while the match is active
- Automatically removed after the match ends
- Not linked to any personal account, email, or stable identifier

For more information, see [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

#### Google Play Billing
The in-app purchase "Remove Ads" is processed by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or receive your payment details — Google Play handles the transaction and only reports the purchase status back to the App.

## Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do not require account creation or login
- We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, or real names
- We do not collect precise location data
- We do not access contacts, photos, or media
- We do not track users across other apps or websites
- We do not sell any user data to third parties

## Data Storage
- **Game statistics** (wins / losses / draws, selected difficulty, language, sound setting, purchase status): Stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to this data.
- **Bluetooth match data:** Exchanged peer-to-peer between the two devices; never transmitted to our servers.
- **Online match data:** Stored on Firebase servers only for the duration of the match and automatically deleted afterwards.

## Children's Privacy
The App is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Online and Bluetooth modes require only an optional display name (which can be any nickname) and no other personal information.

## Your Rights
- You can delete all local data (statistics, preferences) by clearing the app data or uninstalling the App.
- You can reset your advertising ID through your device settings.
- You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.
