# Privacy Policy

**App:** TicTacHelmets
**Developer:** Ganaj
**Last updated:** May 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how "TicTacHelmets" ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application distributed through the Samsung Galaxy Store.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Player nickname:** When you play online or Bluetooth-nearby matches, you may enter a display name. This name is transmitted to the other player only for the duration of the match and is not tied to any real-world identity.
- **Room code:** Online matches use a short, randomly-generated room code. The code exists on our servers only while the match is active.
- **Move data:** During an online or Bluetooth match, the positions of your moves are exchanged with your opponent so both players see the same board.

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Bluetooth scanning:** When you choose the "Nearby" game mode, the App scans for nearby Bluetooth Low Energy devices running TicTacHelmets. No persistent device identifiers are stored; scan results are used only to show the list of discoverable opponents and are discarded when you leave the mode.
- **Approximate location (Bluetooth mode only):** Android requires location permission to perform Bluetooth LE scans on older versions. The App does not read, store, or transmit your location — the permission is requested solely to unblock Bluetooth discovery.
- **Advertising identifier & ad data:** When ads are shown, our ad partners may collect your advertising ID, IP address, general (country/region-level) location, and app usage data. See "Third-Party Services" below.

## Permissions Used

| Permission | Why it is used |
|---|---|
| Internet / Network access | Online multiplayer, advertising, and consent management |
| Advertising ID (AD_ID) | Serving and measuring ads |
| Bluetooth (scan, connect, advertise, admin) | Local "Nearby" peer-to-peer matches |
| Approximate location | Required by Android only to enable Bluetooth scanning; location is never read or stored |

Offline modes (single-player vs. the computer and local two-player on one device) work without an internet connection.

## Third-Party Services

### Google AdMob (and AppLovin mediation)
The App displays advertisements (banner-free interstitial and rewarded video ads) provided by Google AdMob, which may use the AppLovin network through ad mediation. These partners may collect and process certain information, including:
- Device identifiers (advertising ID)
- IP address
- General location (country/region level)
- App usage data

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [AppLovin's Privacy Policy](https://www.applovin.com/privacy/).

### Consent Management (Google UMP)
In regions where it is required (for example, the EEA and the UK), the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the App or your device's ad settings.

### Firebase Realtime Database
Online (remote) multiplayer match data — room code, player nicknames, and move history — is stored on Firebase Realtime Database so both opponents share a consistent view of the board. This data is:
- Stored only while the match is active
- Automatically removed after the match ends
- Not linked to any personal account, email, or stable identifier

For more information, see [Firebase's Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

### In-App Purchases
The App offers a "Remove Ads" in-app purchase, processed by the billing system of the store from which you installed the App (Samsung In-App Purchase on the Samsung Galaxy Store). We do not collect or receive your payment details — the store handles the transaction and only reports the purchase status back to the App. Users who purchase "Remove Ads" no longer see ads after purchase.

## Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do not require account creation or login
- We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, or real names
- We do not collect precise location data
- We do not access contacts, photos, or media
- We do not track users across other apps or websites
- We do not sell any user data to third parties

## Data Storage
- **Game statistics** (wins / losses / draws, selected difficulty, language, sound setting, purchase status): Stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to this data.
- **Bluetooth match data:** Exchanged peer-to-peer between the two devices; never transmitted to our servers.
- **Online match data:** Stored on Firebase servers only for the duration of the match and automatically deleted afterwards.

## Children's Privacy
The App is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Online and Bluetooth modes require only an optional display name (which can be any nickname) and no other personal information.

## Your Rights
- You can delete all local data (statistics, preferences) by clearing the app data or uninstalling the App.
- You can reset your advertising ID through your device settings.
- You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings or via the in-app consent options.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.
